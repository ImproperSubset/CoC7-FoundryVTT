# Diary

## 2026-11-27: hh-rules moved to v0.31.0

The project moved from hh-rules v0.30.0 to v0.31.0, in an update round
hh-advise-hh-rules ran and supervised, started with `hh-background --update`. It
gains the `rule-load-test` rule, now always included, and the `hh-push-down`
skill; the `principles`, `diary` and `glossary` rules and the `hh-dedupe`,
`hh-drift` and `hh-wrap` skills changed, and the copied scripts were brought to
the tag, `.hh/shared/hh-diary-merge` among them. `prek.toml` gained the base's
`minimum_prek_version`, 0.4.5, against this machine's 0.5.11, and the comment
above the jscpd hook was brought to the tag's words by the update itself.
`.gitattributes` is upstream's and was left as it is, as the fork route has it.

That comment was the only step of the v0.30.0 migration item, so the update
removed the item from the plan as done. One migration item went in, **Migrate to
hh-rules v0.31.0**, to run `/hh-push-down` once; the note's other steps found
nothing in the fork's `CLAUDE.md` or its own rule to change, and no test hook
running Vitest or pytest.

hh-advise-hh-rules, supervising, agreed both plan changes and put the new item
below the three `New duplication` lines, where `/hh-rules` places it, rather
than above them, as first proposed.
