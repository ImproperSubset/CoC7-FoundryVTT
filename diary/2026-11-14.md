## 2026-11-14: the open-branches rule in hh-rules

After the setup commit, hh-advise changed `/hh-rules` at hh-rules `c5f8db4`: on
the fork route a branch that changes only upstream's files, such as a `develop`
kept equal to upstream's or a branch held for a pull request upstream, is left

under that rule with no exception.

recorded as hh-advise's decision, made under `/hh-supervise`; the operator had
not confirmed in this session that the advisor's word stands for theirs. 8 files

- `chat-dropdown.js`: 9.2's Foundry 13 code, with the fork's fallback to
  `#chat-notifications` for cards shown as chat notifications.
- `roll-normalize.js`: the fork's exemption for weapon rolls on opposed and
  combined cards, with 9.2's `ui.notifications.error` format option.
- `chat-combat-ranged.js`: the fork (`610d78f0`) and upstream (`08dce7c5`) fixed
  shotgun range damage and difficulty alike; 9.2's form was taken.

  fork's checkbox icons.
- `coc7-chat-message.less`: 9.2's layout, with the fork's green selected state
  on 9.2's `&.switched-on` block.

Adapted beyond the conflicts, for 9.2's renames: the fork's three Prone pool
reasons in `constants.js` from `name` to `label`; the pool rows in
`melee-target.hbs` and the selected-pool tags in `range-initiator.hbs` from

Prone test reads a row's `value`.

Checks: `npm test` 90 of 90, upstream's ESLint with no warnings, and the full
