## 2026-11-16: Keeper hold on combat cards built

The operator's players were rolling from combat cards before the Keeper had set
them up. A CoC7 setting, Hold combat cards for the Keeper, now starts each new
melee and ranged card held (81f451bd): players see it with their rolls disabled
until the Keeper presses Release to players. Upstream's develop has nothing like
it; the melee card's reveal state was the shape copied.

Tests: 10 new in `test/keeper-hold.test.js`, written after the code; hh-watch
found the order, and with the code set aside 8 of them failed, the other 2
checking what was open before the hold. The prone tests' card builders moved to
`test/combat-cards.js` for both files. 103 tests pass. In the local 14.369
runtime's `coc7e` world, a script checked the Keeper's side of both cards, and
the operator checked the player's side in a browser: "That looks like it works."
A first try showed no roll for the player because the card's roller was not the
player's character, which is upstream's rule.

hh-watch also found the setting scope's reason resting on Foundry's permissions
from memory; Foundry 14's source confirmed it. The local runtime's CoC7 went
from release 8.16.hh.8 to a branch build for the trial, which hh-watch raised
against the release doc's rule.

## 2026-11-16: CC_DIARY_DECISION: combat cards held for the Keeper

- Options considered: the card whispered to the Keeper alone by roll mode, then
  revealed; the card shown to all with players' roll buttons disabled until
  released.
- Chosen: the card shown with buttons disabled, because, in the operator's
  words, "it allows players to see that their button press did something. That
  prevents them from spamming the attack."
- Rejected: whispering by roll mode, because a card a player creates uses that
  player's roll mode, and the system has no step to hand a whispered card over
  to the players afterwards.
- Commits: 81f451bd, and the commit that adds this entry

## 2026-11-16: CC_DIARY_DECISION: what a held combat card holds

- Options considered: the attacker's roll alone; the attacker's roll and the
  defender's responses on the melee target card; the roll buttons on both melee
  cards, with the defender's response choice left open.
- Chosen: the roll buttons on both cards, and on the ranged card Shoot as well
  as Roll, since Shoot fixes a shot's dice when pressed; because the Keeper also
  sets the defender's dice, and the target card shows the dice for a response
  only once the player has picked it. The operator: "Yes. That's the right thing
  to do."
- Rejected: the attacker's roll alone, because a defending player could roll
  before the Keeper sets their dice; holding the responses too, because the
  player could not pick a response while held, so the Keeper would have to pick
  it or release before the dice are set.
- Commits: 81f451bd, and the commit that adds this entry

## 2026-11-16: CC_DIARY_DECISION: the Keeper hold setting's scope

- Options considered: a CoC7 setting of world scope; one of client scope.
- Chosen: world scope, shown among the system's settings on its Configure
  Settings tab and kept in each world, because a player cannot switch the hold
  off for themselves, and each world sets it apart. Foundry 14's
  `common/documents/setting.mjs` lets a world setting be changed only by a user
  holding `SETTINGS_MODIFY`, which by default is the GM and Assistant roles, not
  players. The operator: "That's fine."
- Rejected: client scope, because each player would set their own.
- Commits: 81f451bd, and the commit that adds this entry

## 2026-11-16: CC_DIARY_DECISION: Keeper-made combat cards held too

- Options considered: every card held, whoever creates it; only cards a player
  creates held.
- Chosen: every card held, because a card the Keeper creates for an NPC's attack
  opens the defending player's response card, whose roll could otherwise be made
  before the Keeper sets the defender's dice. The operator: "Let's give it a
  try."
- Rejected: only player-made cards held, because it leaves that case open; its
  saving, one press of Release, is small.
- Commits: 81f451bd, and the commit that adds this entry
