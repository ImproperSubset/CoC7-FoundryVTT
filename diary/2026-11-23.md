## 2026-11-23: CC_DIARY_DECISION: a weapon's own Active Effects

- The operator, settling the task with hh-advise: the Flame Pistol gives a
  penalty die when humans fire it, so "I need some way to attach that penalty
  die to an item. Perhaps using an Active Effect?", and "We just need to be able
  to activate and deactivate the penalty die for an instance of the weapon"; the
  design below was agreed with "yes".
- Options considered: apply a weapon's non-transferring effects to the weapon
  itself, as dnd5e's `Item#applyActiveEffects` does; apply them for every item
  type; give the weapon a saved `bonusDice` schema field.
- Chosen: the item applies its enabled effects with "apply to actor" unchecked
  in `prepareEmbeddedDocuments`, for weapons only, on Foundry 13 through the
  effect's `apply` and on 14 through the static `applyChange`. The weapon holds
  `bonusDice` in memory with a getter and setter, as skills do, reset to 0 in
  `prepareBaseData`, and the combat cards already add it to the pool. The weapon
  sheet gets the Effects tab the armor sheet has.
- Rejected: every item type, because other types' non-transferring effects have
  never applied, and existing ones, such as an armor effect keyed to an actor's
  skill with "apply to actor" unchecked, would start writing into the item; a
  saved field, because the weapon's saved data stays as upstream's.
- Commits: 8a624744
