## 2026-11-19: hh-rules moved to v0.26.0

The project moved from hh-rules v0.25.0 to v0.26.0, in an update round hh-advise
ran under the operator's `/hh-supervise`. It gains no rule, skill or pack; the
`principles` rule gains that project sessions may message each other directly,
with requests for work still as issues, and that a release needs the operator's
go-ahead, asked once at the wrap. The copied scripts, the commit hooks and the
tool configs already matched the tag. No migration item went into the plan: the
v0.26.0 note's steps all say there is nothing to do.
